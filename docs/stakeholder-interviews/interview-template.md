# Stakeholder Interview Template (15 Minutes)

## Objective

Validate whether methodology transparency and divergence warnings are useful, understandable, and safe in patient-facing ER wait-time tooling.

## Timeboxed Agenda

1. Intro and context (2 min)
2. Core questions (10 min)
3. Wrap-up and permission check (3 min)

## Intro Script

Thanks for taking the time.
This interview is validating a wait-time transparency tool that flags invalid comparisons when provinces use different measurement methods.
The goal is to understand whether the framing is useful and what should be changed before broader launch.

## Core Questions

1. When patients ask about ER wait times, what do they usually misunderstand?
2. If you saw a warning saying two hospitals are not directly comparable due to methodology, would that be clear and useful?
3. What minimum context should patients see before acting on wait-time data?
4. Could any current wording lead to confusion or harm?
5. What one improvement would make this most useful for your workflow or for patient education?
