📋 Ontology Field Definitions

Field Definition Implications
START_EVENT When the wait time clock starts TRIAGE starts after clinical assessment; REGISTRATION starts after administrative check-in (earlier)
END_EVENT When the wait time clock stops PHYSICIAN = first physician assessment (standard across all provinces)
STATISTIC_TYPE How the value is calculated MEAN = arithmetic average; POINT_ESTIMATE = current/instantaneous; P90 = 90th percentile; ROLLING_AVG = moving average
