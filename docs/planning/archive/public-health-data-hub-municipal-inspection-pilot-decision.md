# Public Health Hub Municipal Inspection Pilot Decision

**Created:** 2026-03-28
**Status:** Archived decision (closed 2026-04-24)
**Scope:** Whether Wait Time Canada should pursue a Toronto-first municipal
inspection/compliance pilot as the next public-health-hub expansion step
**Related:** `../roadmap.md`, `../post-launch-growth-strategy.md`

## Summary

Decision: **do not pursue a Toronto-only municipal inspection/compliance pilot
now.**

The data is credible, but the current `/resources` direction remains
Ontario-first, emergency-access-adjacent, and intentionally resistant to
city-by-city connector sprawl. The municipal inspection pilot remains a future
candidate only if a broader Ontario strategy or explicit product-direction
change emerges.

## Why It Was Closed

1. The best validated inspection datasets are municipal, not province-wide.
2. A Toronto-only sidecar would fragment the current Ontario-first product
   story.
3. The near-term `/resources` path is stronger when it stays focused on
   facilities, alerts, AQHI, system context, water advisories, and explicit
   official link-outs.

## Re-evaluation Trigger

Reopen only if at least one of these becomes true:

1. another Ontario municipal or regional inspection dataset is validated at a
   comparable quality level
2. a broader Ontario inspection connector strategy is intentionally drafted
3. the product direction broadens beyond the current emergency-access-adjacent
   framing
