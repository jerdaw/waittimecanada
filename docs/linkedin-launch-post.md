# LinkedIn Launch Post - Wait Time Canada

## Final Recommended Post (Ready To Publish)

Wait Time Canada is now live: https://wait-time.ca

Emergency department wait times in Canada are often presented as if they are directly comparable. They are not.

**Wait Time Canada** is a public-interest health systems observatory built to make those differences visible instead of flattening them into a simple ranking.

What it does:
- Tags each measurement with explicit methodology metadata
- Flags invalid cross-system comparisons with divergence warnings
- Documents source methods, limitations, and data-quality context
- Provides an Ontario public resources module with facility search, AED fallback data, Health Canada alerts, AQHI, and an official naloxone link-out

Why this matters:
- Public health information is often fragmented, inconsistently defined, and hard to compare
- Those gaps create real barriers for patients, clinicians, researchers, journalists, and policy users
- Better access only helps if the limits and methodology are visible too

Current scope:
- 4 provinces
- 393 hospital records across the active source inventories
- 8,804 measurements in the most recent 24-hour quality window
- 6 of 6 cross-province source pairs currently fail direct comparability on the platform's ontology tags

The goal is straightforward: improve access to operational health-system information, reduce misleading comparisons, and make the reporting context easier to understand.

Live demo: https://wait-time.ca
GitHub: https://github.com/jerdaw/waittimecanada
